# Brand Identity: Color Palette & Typography

To ensure **Shelby The Barista** resonates with modern culture while retaining an elegant, highly relatable aesthetic, we have developed a sophisticated visual identity. This identity bridges the gap between raw street culture (the "Fourth Space" sidewalk vibe) and premium, curated coffee craftsmanship.

It balances **70% Gen-Z Minimalism** with **30% Brutalist Edge**, primarily expressed through contrasting typography.

## 🎨 Color Palette

Our palette is designed to be highly legible on digital screens while evoking the physical experience of visiting the café.

| Color Name | HEX Code | RGB | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Espresso Noir** | `#1A1513` | `26, 21, 19` | Primary text, bold backgrounds, and logo marks. Represents the core product (coffee) and provides high-contrast elegance. |
| **Crema White** | `#F8F5F2` | `248, 245, 242` | Primary background color. Softer than pure white, reducing eye strain and evoking the warmth of steamed milk and cozy aesthetics. |
| **Dopamine Blue** | `#1A5BFF` | `26, 91, 255` | Primary accent color. A vibrant, electric blue that references the viral "Blue Dopamine" drink. |
| **Sidewalk Grey** | `#8A8D91` | `138, 141, 145` | Secondary text, borders, and subtle UI elements. References the concrete and the street culture that forms the café's "Fourth Space." |
| **Hazelnut Gold** | `#CBA052` | `203, 160, 82` | Secondary accent. Used sparingly for premium badges, stars, or special menu item highlights. |

## 🔤 Typography & Contrast

The typography strategy is where the 30% brutalist edge comes in. We rely on extreme contrast between clean, elegant utility and aggressive styling.

### 1. Primary Brand Font (SHELBY): **Inter**, **Outfit**, or **Space Grotesk**
* **Style**: 70% Gen-Z Minimalist.
* **Usage**: The word "SHELBY", main headings, UI Elements.
* **Vibe**: Clean, elegant, geometric, and highly readable. It gives off a tech-forward, modern vibe.

### 2. Secondary Sub-Brand Font (the barista): **"The Boys" Style Block Font**
* **Style**: 30% Brutalist Edge.
* **Usage**: The words "the barista" in the logo, and for specific disruptive marketing campaigns.
* **Vibe**: An aggressive, slightly distressed, italicized block font. It brings a raw, authentic energy that disrupts the clean minimalism of "SHELBY", proving it's not just a sterile corporate cafe.

## ✒️ Logo & Visual Language
* **The Logo**: The word **SHELBY** dominates the space in a clean, elegant sans-serif (Espresso Noir color on Crema White). Directly beneath it, **the barista** is stamped in the aggressive, tilted block font. 
* **Vibe**: The overall aesthetic remains premium and highly Instagrammable, but the clash of fonts gives it that subtle "hole-in-the-wall" street credibility.
